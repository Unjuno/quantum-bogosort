\section{Appendix Roadmap}

The final appendix is expected to contain:

\begin{enumerate}
\item full proofs of Theorems 1--5;
\item strict/equality conditions for FOSD;
\item adaptive-rescue covariance proof;
\item binary soft-QBS derivation;
\item repeated-filter sensitivity identity;
\item Gaussian closed-form derivation;
\item multi-observer normalization examples;
\item additional robustness experiments and archived parameter sweeps;
\item reproducibility details and random-seed conventions.
\end{enumerate}

During public review, the detailed material remains in the repository theorem and supplementary notes so corrections can be made before the manuscript appendix is frozen.
