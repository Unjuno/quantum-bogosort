\section{Residual-Variance Certificate}

This section records Supplementary Theorem S2.12, a variance-based lower certificate for the residual term in S2.11.

Let
\[
m(Y)=\mathbb E[U\mid Y],
\qquad
a(Y)=\mathbb E[S\mid Y],
\]
\[
v_U(Y)=\operatorname{Var}(U\mid Y),
\qquad
v_S(Y)=\operatorname{Var}(S\mid Y).
\]
Assume $U$ and $S$ are square-integrable, with $S\ge0$ and $0<\mathbb E[S]<\infty$.

\begin{theorem}[Residual-variance lower certificate]
The total covariance satisfies
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)
\ge
\operatorname{Cov}(m(Y),a(Y))
-
\mathbb E\!\left[
\sqrt{v_U(Y)v_S(Y)}
\right].
\]
Furthermore,
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)
\ge
\operatorname{Cov}(m(Y),a(Y))
-
\sqrt{
\mathbb E[v_U(Y)]
\mathbb E[v_S(Y)]
}.
\]
Hence either conditional-mean alignment margin exceeding the corresponding residual-variance penalty is sufficient for positive total covariance.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
S2.11 gives
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)
=
\operatorname{Cov}(m(Y),a(Y))
+
\mathbb E[\operatorname{Cov}(U,S\mid Y)].
\]
Conditional Cauchy--Schwarz implies
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S\mid Y)
\ge
-
\sqrt{
\operatorname{Var}(U\mid Y)
\operatorname{Var}(S\mid Y)
}.
\]
Taking expectations yields the first lower bound. Applying ordinary Cauchy--Schwarz to $\sqrt{v_U(Y)}$ and $\sqrt{v_S(Y)}$ gives
\[
\mathbb E[\sqrt{v_U(Y)v_S(Y)}]
\le
\sqrt{\mathbb E[v_U(Y)]\mathbb E[v_S(Y)]},
\]
which yields the second bound.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Residual-energy form.}
With
\[
\eta=U-m(Y),
\qquad
\xi=S-a(Y),
\]
we have
\[
\mathbb E[v_U(Y)]=\mathbb E[\eta^2],
\qquad
\mathbb E[v_S(Y)]=\mathbb E[\xi^2],
\]
so
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)
\ge
\operatorname{Cov}(m(Y),a(Y))
-
\sqrt{\mathbb E[\eta^2]\mathbb E[\xi^2]}.
\]

\paragraph{Sharpness.}
The conditional Cauchy--Schwarz penalty is sharp. With constant $Y$ and centered residuals $\xi=-c\eta$ for $c>0$, the residual covariance equals the negative conditional standard-deviation product. Adding a sufficiently large constant to $S$ preserves nonnegativity without changing covariance. Thus conditional variances alone cannot yield a uniformly tighter universal residual lower bound.
