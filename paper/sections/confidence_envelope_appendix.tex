\section{Supplementary Corollary S2.8: Generic Confidence-Envelope Certificate}

S2.5 uses bounded observations and Hoeffding concentration. The covariance-composition argument itself only requires simultaneous confidence envelopes for a small collection of population moments.

Assume $S\ge0$, $0<\mathbb E[S]<\infty$, and finite required moments. Suppose a statistical procedure produces random bounds such that, with probability at least $1-\delta$,
\[
L_Y\le\mathbb E[Y]\le U_Y,
\qquad
L_S\le\mathbb E[S]\le U_S,
\]
\[
\mathbb E[YS]\ge L_{YS},
\qquad
\mathbb E[S^2]\le U_{S^2},
\qquad
\mathbb E[(U-Y)^2]\le U_M.
\]
Define
\[
L_S^+=\max\{0,L_S\}
\]
and
\[
P_U
=
\max\{L_YL_S^+,L_YU_S,U_YL_S^+,U_YU_S\}.
\]
Let
\[
C_L=L_{YS}-P_U,
\qquad
V_U=\max\{0,U_{S^2}-(L_S^+)^2\},
\]
and define
\[
D_{\mathrm{env}}
=
C_L-\sqrt{U_MV_U}.
\]

\begin{corollary}[Generic confidence-envelope certificate]
Under the simultaneous confidence event,
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)
\ge
D_{\mathrm{env}}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
P\!\left(
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)
\ge D_{\mathrm{env}}
\right)
\ge1-\delta.
\]
If $D_{\mathrm{env}}>0$ and $0<U_S<\infty$, then on the same event
\[
\mathbb E_{\mathrm{FP}}[U]-\mathbb E[U]
\ge
\frac{D_{\mathrm{env}}}{U_S}
>0.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
On the simultaneous confidence event,
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(Y,S)
=
\mathbb E[YS]-\mathbb E[Y]\mathbb E[S].
\]
Because $S\ge0$,
\[
\mathbb E[S]\in[L_S^+,U_S].
\]
The bilinear map $(a,b)\mapsto ab$ achieves its maximum over the rectangle
\[
[L_Y,U_Y]\times[L_S^+,U_S]
\]
at a corner, so
\[
\mathbb E[Y]\mathbb E[S]
\le P_U.
\]
Hence
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(Y,S)
\ge L_{YS}-P_U
=C_L.
\]
Furthermore,
\[
\operatorname{Var}(S)
=
\mathbb E[S^2]-\mathbb E[S]^2
\le
U_{S^2}-(L_S^+)^2
\le V_U,
\]
where the outer maximum only enforces a nonnegative reported envelope outside the simultaneous-validity event. Also
\[
\mathbb E[(U-Y)^2]\le U_M.
\]
S2.4 therefore gives
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)
\ge
C_L-\sqrt{U_MV_U}
=D_{\mathrm{env}}.
\]
The coverage statement follows from the assumed probability of the simultaneous event. If $D_{\mathrm{env}}>0$, then T1 and $\mathbb E[S]\le U_S$ give
\[
\mathbb E_{\mathrm{FP}}[U]-\mathbb E[U]
=
\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)}{\mathbb E[S]}
\ge
\frac{D_{\mathrm{env}}}{U_S}.
\]
\end{proof}

\subsection{Modularity}

S2.5 is one instantiation of this result using bounded Hoeffding envelopes. Any alternative procedure that genuinely supplies the required simultaneous moment coverage can be substituted without changing the covariance-composition proof. Possible statistical layers include sub-Gaussian or sub-exponential concentration, empirical Bernstein bounds, robust mean estimators, or confidence sequences, subject to their own assumptions and coverage guarantees.

The theorem does not assert that any particular statistical method is valid outside its assumptions. It only composes a valid simultaneous moment envelope into a covariance lower certificate.
