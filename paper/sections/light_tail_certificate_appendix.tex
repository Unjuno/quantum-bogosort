\section{Light-Tail Confidence-Envelope Instantiation}

This section records Corollary S2.9, an unbounded light-tail instantiation of the generic S2.8 confidence-envelope certificate.

Let
\[
R=U-Y.
\]
Assume $S\ge0$ and $0<\mathbb E[S]<\infty$.

For an i.i.d. sample of size $n$, suppose $Y$ and $S$ satisfy the two-sided sample-mean concentration bounds
\[
P\!\left(
|\bar X-\mathbb E[X]|>
\sigma_X\sqrt{\frac{2t}{n}}
\right)
\le2e^{-t},
\qquad X\in\{Y,S\}.
\]
Suppose further that each of
\[
YS,\qquad S^2,\qquad R^2
\]
satisfies a Bernstein-type mean bound
\[
P\!\left(
|\bar W-\mathbb E[W]|>
\sqrt{\frac{2v_Wt}{n}}+
\frac{b_Wt}{n}
\right)
\le2e^{-t}.
\]

\begin{corollary}[Light-tail S2.8 instantiation]
Let
\[
t_\delta=\log\frac{10}{\delta}.
\]
Define
\[
r_Y=\sigma_Y\sqrt{\frac{2t_\delta}{n}},
\qquad
r_S=\sigma_S\sqrt{\frac{2t_\delta}{n}},
\]
\[
r_{YS}=\sqrt{\frac{2v_{YS}t_\delta}{n}}+\frac{b_{YS}t_\delta}{n},
\]
\[
r_{S^2}=\sqrt{\frac{2v_{S^2}t_\delta}{n}}+\frac{b_{S^2}t_\delta}{n},
\]
and
\[
r_{R^2}=\sqrt{\frac{2v_{R^2}t_\delta}{n}}+\frac{b_{R^2}t_\delta}{n}.
\]
Construct
\[
L_Y=\bar Y-r_Y,
\quad U_Y=\bar Y+r_Y,
\quad
L_S=\bar S-r_S,
\quad U_S=\bar S+r_S,
\]
\[
L_{YS}=\overline{YS}-r_{YS},
\quad
U_{S^2}=\overline{S^2}+r_{S^2},
\quad
U_M=\overline{R^2}+r_{R^2}.
\]
Let
\[
L_S^+=\max\{0,L_S\},
\]
\[
P_U=
\max\{L_YL_S^+,L_YU_S,U_YL_S^+,U_YU_S\},
\]
\[
C_L=L_{YS}-P_U,
\qquad
V_U=\max\{0,U_{S^2}-(L_S^+)^2\},
\]
and
\[
D_{\rm LT}=C_L-\sqrt{U_MV_U}.
\]
Then
\[
P\!\left(
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)\ge D_{\rm LT}
\right)
\ge1-\delta.
\]
Hence $D_{\rm LT}>0$ certifies positive population covariance at confidence at least $1-\delta$. If $0<U_S<\infty$ on the same simultaneous event, then
\[
\mathbb E_{\rm FP}[U]-\mathbb E[U]
\ge
\frac{D_{\rm LT}}{U_S}>0.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Set $t=t_\delta$. By assumption, each of the five moment-concentration events fails with probability at most
\[
2e^{-t_\delta}=\frac{\delta}{5}.
\]
A union bound therefore gives simultaneous coverage at least $1-\delta$ for
\[
L_Y\le\mathbb E[Y]\le U_Y,
\qquad
L_S\le\mathbb E[S]\le U_S,
\]
\[
\mathbb E[YS]\ge L_{YS},
\qquad
\mathbb E[S^2]\le U_{S^2},
\qquad
\mathbb E[(U-Y)^2]\le U_M.
\]
These are exactly the S2.8 inputs. Applying the deterministic S2.8 composition yields
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(U,S)
\ge D_{\rm LT}
\]
on the simultaneous event, proving the coverage statement. The first-person lower bound follows from T1 and the simultaneous upper bound $\mathbb E[S]\le U_S$ when $D_{\rm LT}>0$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Selection-safe extension.}
For $K$ candidates fixed independently of the certification data, predeclare failure budgets $\delta_k$ satisfying
\[
\sum_{k=1}^K\delta_k\le\delta.
\]
Applying S2.9 to candidate $k$ with
\[
t_k=\log\frac{10}{\delta_k}
\]
and then taking a union bound gives simultaneous validity for all candidates. Thus any subsequent data-dependent choice among the predeclared candidates retains its own valid lower bound, as in S2.7.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The theorem takes the concentration controls for $YS$, $S^2$, and $(U-Y)^2$ as explicit inputs. Marginal light-tail control of $Y$ and $S$ alone is not silently promoted to universal product/square constants. Tail parameters estimated from the same certification sample require additional accounting. Finite variance alone does not imply the concentration assumptions used here.
